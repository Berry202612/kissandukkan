# KissanDukkan product photo guide

Use this guide when you photograph products yourself. Official pack shots from distributors or brand offices are also fine (send them `scripts/output/image-request-list.csv`). **Never** download photos from other online stores, and never create "fake" branded pack images.

## Set-up
- **Background:** plain white sweep (a sheet of white chart paper curved from the wall onto the table works well). No textured cloth, no props.
- **Light:** soft daylight from a window (no direct sun), or two softbox / LED lights at 45° on each side. Avoid the camera flash – it causes glare on glossy labels.
- **Camera:** any recent phone is enough. Clean the lens. Use the main (1×) lens, not wide-angle. Hold steady or use a small tripod.

## Shots for every product
1. **Front** – full pack, label straight, filling about 80% of the frame.
2. **Back label** – technical content, dosage table, batch/expiry area, manufacturer details. Every word must be readable when zoomed.
3. **Side** – shows depth and any side text (registration number, caution symbol).
4. Optional: cap/seal close-up, pack next to a hand for size, contents (for tools and kits only).

## Output
- **Size:** 1600 × 1600 px, square. Product centred with even white margin.
- **Format:** JPG (quality 85) or WebP. Under 5 MB.
- **Editing:** crop, straighten and adjust brightness only. **No filters, no beauty effects, no added text, no logos, no watermarks.** Do not edit the label in any way.
- **File name:** `brand-product-packsize-front.jpg`, e.g. `acme-neemoil-1500ppm-500ml-front.jpg`.

## Upload
Admin → Products → open the product → **Images** → upload (first image = main image) → set the source to **Own photo** or **Distributor / brand pack shot** → **Save images**. The launch checklist turns green once every published product has a real image.
